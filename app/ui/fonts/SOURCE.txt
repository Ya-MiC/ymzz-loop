Noto Sans SC variable slices in this directory are unmodified woff2 files
downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com on 2026-11-10. They are the weight-axis
slices Google Fonts serves for Noto Sans SC, limited to the ranges the
window uses. Other glyphs fall back to the next installed face.

The license is OFL.txt, copied from
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notosanssc/OFL.txt
The copyright line in that file names Adobe Source Han Sans. Noto Sans SC
is the Google Fonts name of that family. These bytes were not renamed.
