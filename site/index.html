        04 — FAQ

        Questions

        Is it really free?

        Yes. It runs on GitHub's free Actions minutes and free Notion/Google APIs. There is no paid tier.

        Where is my data?

        In your Notion, your Google account and your repository. This website stores language, theme and setup progress in your browser (localStorage). Demo to-dos on this page use a 60-hour first-party cookie, then disappear. No trackers.

        What if a run fails?

        You get an email with a link to the failed run. The next hour retries automatically, because a day is only marked done after success.

        Will it run forever?

        By design: first success starts a 600-day chapter (countdown 600→0). At 0 the daily loop pauses and the chapter is meant to close with a 600-day summary. Your Notion, Sheets and Markdown stay yours. Deploy a newer ymzz-loop version to open the next 600-day chapter.

        Can I change the template?

        Yes — every field is defined in one file, scripts/schema.mjs.

        What do I need before setup?

        A GitHub account (fork the product branch), a Notion workspace you can connect an integration to, a Google account for Sheets and the reminder email, and about 10 minutes (see the start guide). No paid plan required on any of them.

        Is this website the product?

        No. This page is the showcase. The automation lives on the product-only GitHub branch — fork or download that branch so you do not copy the website into your repo.

        Why can I add to-dos on today’s page?

        It is a browser-only demo of slots 03–08 (up to 6 items). They stay in this browser via localStorage and a 60-hour cookie, then disappear. Nothing is sent to a server.

        How did you compare Day One, Zapier, and Obsidian?

        Qualitatively, from public product docs as of November 2026 — not from speed tests or invented user counts. See Day One pricing, Zapier pricing, and Obsidian pricing. We compare ownership, cost model, and whether yesterday is ingested automatically.
